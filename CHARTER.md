# PARALLAX CONGRESS
## Founding Charter
### AI & Technology

**Motto: Join the Congress.**

### Article I — Mission

Parallax Congress is a democratic advisory institution dedicated to advancing competition, innovation, accountability, and public participation in artificial intelligence and technology.

Congress exists to give individuals, businesses, developers, researchers, and communities a meaningful voice in shaping the technologies that increasingly govern economic and civic life.

We believe concentrated corporate power must be challenged through competition, independent innovation, open participation, and informed public policy.

### Article II — Founding Principles

Parallax Congress operates according to six principles:

1. **Competition.** Promote fair markets and challenge practices that prevent meaningful competition.
2. **Democracy.** Every member has an equal vote, regardless of wealth, influence, or financial contribution.
3. **Independence.** Defend the ability to build, own, operate, and choose technology independently.
4. **Transparency.** Make proposals, decisions, financial interests, and institutional activities publicly accountable.
5. **Innovation.** Encourage open standards, interoperability, research, and technological advancement.
6. **Responsibility.** Advocate for technology that benefits people, businesses, and society.

### Article III — Membership

Membership is open to individuals who support the mission and agree to the charter.

**Public Membership — Free**

Public members may:

- Participate in discussions and public proceedings.
- Submit proposals and policy recommendations.
- Vote on eligible matters.
- Participate in open working groups.
- Seek elected representative or committee positions.

**Advisory Seat — $1,000 per Month**

Paid advisory members receive all public membership rights, along with:

- Participation in dedicated strategic advisory sessions.
- Access to Parallax leadership consultations.
- Participation in specialized industry and policy working groups.
- Opportunities to present research, technical expertise, and institutional proposals.

Businesses may sponsor an identified individual for an advisory seat.

Payment does not confer additional votes, ownership, fiduciary authority, privileged policy outcomes, or the right to direct Congress.

**One person. One vote.**

### Article IV — Democratic Governance

Congress practices direct democracy.

Members may introduce proposals concerning technology, infrastructure, policy, competition, and institutional priorities.

Proposals undergo an open discussion period before voting. Ordinary recommendations pass by a simple majority of votes cast.

The Congress shall publish voting rules, participation totals, decisions, and relevant conflicts of interest.

Members may elect representatives and establish committees for research, policy development, and institutional administration. Such bodies remain accountable to the membership.

Membership voting rights belong to verified individuals, not corporate entities or automated accounts.

### Article V — Advisory Authority

Parallax Congress is an advisory institution.

Its recommendations inform the strategic direction of Parallax and the advocacy priorities submitted to Fervent Wishes Co.

Congress does not exercise the legal authority of a corporate board of directors.

Parallax leadership retains responsibility for corporate operations, finances, contracts, employees, intellectual property, and legal compliance.

Leadership shall provide a public response to formally adopted recommendations, including the reasons for accepting, modifying, or declining them.

### Article VI — Advocacy and Lobbying

**Fervent Wishes Co. serves as the designated advocacy and lobbying partner of Parallax Congress.**

Its mandate may include:

- Public education and awareness campaigns.
- Legislative and regulatory research.
- Policy development and publication.
- Coalition building and public engagement.
- Lawful lobbying and representation before government institutions.

Advocacy priorities originate through the democratic processes of Congress.

Execution requires appropriate authorization, funding, and a written agreement with Fervent Wishes Co.

Fervent Wishes retains its independent corporate responsibilities and must comply with applicable lobbying, disclosure, and political activity laws.

No member or sponsor may purchase a policy position, legislative endorsement, or lobbying outcome.

### Article VII — Funding and Accountability

Congress may receive revenue from:

- Paid advisory memberships.
- Voluntary contributions.
- Institutional sponsorships.
- Events, publications, and educational programs.
- Other lawful activities consistent with its mission.

The standard advisory membership is **$1,000 per month**, subject to published billing and cancellation terms.

Congress shall disclose material institutional sponsors and publish periodic financial summaries, including advocacy and lobbying expenditures.

Financial contributions shall not determine voting power.

Conflicts of interest must be disclosed. Members exercising institutional authority may be required to recuse themselves from decisions involving direct financial interests.

### Article VIII — Relationship to Parallax

Parallax Web Services, Inc. is the founding sponsor and initial organizer of Parallax Congress.

Parallax may provide administrative support, technology, infrastructure, personnel, and funding.

Congress may advise on the development of Parallax technologies, including distributed computing, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, software, and digital infrastructure.

Congress shall remain free to propose positions that differ from the commercial interests of Parallax or its sponsors.

### Article IX — Amendments

This charter may be amended through a public proposal, a minimum fourteen-day member review period, and approval by two-thirds of votes cast.

Amendments affecting legal obligations, corporate authority, or contractual relationships require review and acceptance by the affected legal entities before taking effect.

### Article X — Establishment

Parallax Congress shall initially operate as a publicly governed advisory initiative convened by Parallax Web Services, Inc.

Its eventual independent legal structure, if established, shall be determined through a transparent organizational process consistent with this charter and applicable law.

**Founding Declaration**

The future of artificial intelligence and technology is too consequential to be shaped exclusively by a handful of powerful institutions.

Parallax Congress invites the public, industry, and independent innovators to participate in developing competitive markets, responsible policy, and technological progress.

**Join the Congress.**
